📊 Myntra Sales Dashboard
An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing Myntra's sales performance across products, categories, payment types, and years — built using Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers, and KPI metrics.

🎯 Objective
To analyze Myntra's retail sales data and extract actionable insights on revenue trends, top-performing products & categories, payment preferences, and year-over-year growth — enabling smarter business decisions.

📌 Key Metrics & Insights
 Metric : Value 
 💰 Total Sales : ₹ 8,74,064 
 📈 Total Profit : ₹ 1,54,420 
 📅 Years Covered : 2024 & 2025 
 
🔍 Insights Uncovered
 🏆 Top Product by Revenue — Kurtas lead with ₹1,57,999, followed by Sarees and ethnic wear
 🗓️ Monthly Revenue Trend — Peak sales in March (₹1,17,558); lowest in November–December
 👗 Top Category — Women's wear drives the highest revenue at ₹2,26,895
 📊 YoY Growth — Total sales grew from ₹4,19,603 (2024) to ₹4,54,461 (2025)

🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
 Tool : Purpose 
 Microsoft Excel : Dashboard creation & visualization 
 Pivot Tables : Data aggregation by product, category, month 
 Pivot Charts : Bar, Line & Pie chart visualizations 
 Slicers : Interactive filtering by Month, Year, Category 
 KPI Cards : Highlighting Total Sales & Total Profit 
 Conditional Formatting : Visual emphasis on key data points
